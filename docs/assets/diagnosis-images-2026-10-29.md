## 生成プロンプト

以下の共通指定と各画像の指定を連結して入力しました。

### 共通指定

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: one 3:2 landscape website card photograph, 1536x1024. A visually coherent series for a Japanese small-business AI improvement service. Refined editorial photography, realistic and practical Japanese office, pale oak desk, soft side daylight, warm ivory, muted sage green and deep navy palette, gentle contrast, crisp central subject readable at thumbnail size. Show meaningful real work, minimal props, believable hands and realistic objects. Crop out faces. No logos, no watermarks, no robot, no glowing technology, no floating icons, no decorative plants or coffee, no growth metaphors. Any small document text must be unobtrusive and fictional. This is an illustrative scene, not a screenshot of a real product. Single photograph, not a collage or triptych.

### diagnosis-review-v2

Primary request: illustrate identifying which everyday work to improve. Slightly overhead close photograph of an office worker in a navy shirt at a desk, actively comparing a small set of printed business documents: a report with paragraphs, a simple expense spreadsheet, and printed email correspondence. Three orderly document groups are visible and visually distinct, with a sage tab marking one priority. One hand points with a navy pencil to a highlighted repetitive row on the spreadsheet; the other holds the short task checklist beside it. The main subject is the act of examining and prioritizing actual work documents, not an empty clipboard. The documents and hands fill the frame, natural carefully composed arrangement, lots of white paper and clear visual hierarchy. No readable headings or numbers required; short gray text lines and believable table structure.

### diagnosis-prompt-v2

Primary request: illustrate preparing a specific instruction for AI using a source document. Over-the-shoulder close editorial photograph, face out of frame, worker's hand on laptop trackpad on a pale oak desk. Laptop is the central subject with screen facing the camera at a readable angle. Screen shows a clean generic AI workspace: a narrow source-document pane on the left with a small attached document preview, and a large structured instruction composer on the right with three clearly separated pale sections for task, conditions, and output format. Use quiet gray short text lines without readable words, one sage highlighted paragraph, a small understated navy send arrow inside the screen. A single printed report with matching highlighted lines lies immediately to the left and the person's other hand points to that source document. The scene should clearly communicate translating real work material into a structured AI instruction, not merely a laptop displaying chat bubbles. No device branding. Photographically believable screen and reflections.

### diagnosis-start-v2

Primary request: illustrate a practical first step with human checking before adoption. Close three-quarter overhead editorial photograph of a worker at the same pale oak desk, checking a printed one-page implementation checklist against an open laptop displaying a draft business document. Printed plan is dominant in the foreground, three short rows of tasks with clear square checkboxes, first two checked in muted sage and the last still open. One hand holds the plan steady and the other holds a navy pen poised above the final review checkbox; show normal human hands with natural anatomy. The laptop occupies the upper left background with a neat document preview, beside a single source paper. Use short gray line text with no readable wording; clear practical documentation not a chart or rising blocks. Communicate prepare materials, try once, then a person verifies the result before using it. Professional, approachable, calm, believable small-business work scene.
